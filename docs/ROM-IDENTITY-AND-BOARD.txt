MEGA MAN 2 ROM IDENTITY AND BOARD NOTES

The accepted ROM is the standard Mega Man 2 (USA) iNES image listed in config/expected_rom.txt. The iNES header declares mapper 1, 16 PRG units and no CHR ROM. This means 256 KiB PRG ROM and CHR RAM.

Public cartridge databases identify the matching PRG CRC32 0FCFC04D as an SGROM-family board with MMC1B2 and 8 KiB VRAM/CHR RAM. The old iNES header cannot encode all board details, so the project binds identity using full-file SHA-256 plus database evidence instead of relying on mapper number alone.

The lsmmega/mm2 disassembly is a valuable symbol and source-layout reference, but its README/header identity must not automatically replace this project's exact-ROM identity. Any imported address, label or rebuilt binary must be compared against the accepted ROM before promotion.
